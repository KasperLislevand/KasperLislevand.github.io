## Background

This visualization explores the consolidation of companies within an industry over time. Mergers and acquisitions reshape market structures in ways that are difficult to grasp from tables alone — a Sankey diagram makes the flow of ownership immediately visible.

## What is a Sankey diagram?

A Sankey diagram is a flow diagram where the width of each arrow is proportional to the quantity it represents. In this context, each flow represents a company being absorbed into a larger entity through acquisition or merger.
